# Model notes (honest limits)
- ET0: FAO-56 Penman-Monteith. Soil: Saxton-Rawls pedotransfer, 4 layers. Water: daily tipping bucket, root-weighted uptake, demand-based irrigation (flood 0.55, sprinkler 0.75, drip 0.90 efficiency).
- Salinity: indicative salt balance, Maas-Hoffman relative yield; exclusion <65%, risk <85%.
- Not modelled: rice paddy ponding, erosion, pests dynamics, nitrogen quantity (qualitative only), prices.
- Heat threshold generic 35 C. Crop parameters are FAO-default seeds, unreviewed.
- Scores are min-max within the candidate pool: relative, not absolute.
